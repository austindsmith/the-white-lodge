# Step 1 — Welcome back

## The White Lodge has moved

Click **Next** to get started.

# Step 2 — Remove the old server

## Important: forget the dead server first

Your Plex apps probably still remember the old server and will show it as "unavailable" forever. Clean it up so you don't end up staring at a broken library:

1. Open Plex on any device
2. Go to **Settings → Manage Libraries** (or the server picker in the top left)
3. If you see a White Lodge server marked offline or unavailable, choose **Forget** or **Remove from list**

On the web app: click your profile picture → **Account Settings → Authorized Devices** and remove anything stale while you're there.

# Step 3 — Connect your account

## Sign in and accept the invite

On the next screen you'll sign in with your existing Plex account (the same email you used before). Once you do, the new server is shared to your account instantly.

No new account needed. If you forgot your Plex password, reset it at plex.tv first, then come back to this page.

# Step 4 — Install Plex on your devices

## Where to watch

Plex runs on basically everything:

- **TV**: Apple TV, Android TV / Google TV, Roku, Fire TV, and most smart TV app stores — search "Plex"
- **Phone / tablet**: App Store or Play Store
- **Computer**: app.plex.tv in any browser, or the desktop app

Sign in with the same account and the White Lodge appears automatically.

### One setting that fixes 90% of quality complaints

In the Plex app go to **Settings → Video Quality** (sometimes under Playback) and set both Home Streaming and Remote Streaming to **Original / Maximum**. Some apps default to 720p and it makes everything look worse than it should.
